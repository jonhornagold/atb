For candidates

Missed out on a place? Your apprenticeship journey isn’t over.

You showed real potential in a competitive apprenticeship process. The Apprenticeship Talent Bridge helps you take that potential to other employers near you who are looking for someone just like you.

Is this for me?

Who can join the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge

The current pilot is for people who applied for an apprenticeship with a participating major defence employer — BAE Systems (Barrow-in-Furness and Preston) or Babcock (Devonport) — and who:

  • reached the agreed stage of that employer’s recruitment process
  • were not offered a role on this occasion
  • would like to explore other apprenticeship opportunities in their local area

If you’re eligible, you’ll be invited to opt in. Joining is entirely your choice.

Not being offered a role doesn’t mean you weren’t good enough

Major defence employers receive far more strong applications than they have apprenticeship places. Reaching the later stages of their process shows you have what employers are looking for — the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge makes sure that doesn’t go unseen.

What you get

Why join the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge?

Genuine, approved roles

Every opportunity is prepared and approved before you’re invited to apply — real apprenticeships with the training arrangements already in place.

Close to home

Choose the opportunities that suit your interests, location and how far you’re able to travel.

A clear timetable

A coordinated campaign means you know when applications open and when employers will shortlist and interview.

Employers you might not know

Discover local organisations offering high-quality apprenticeships that you may never have come across.

Earn while you learn

Fair process

Your journey

How it works for candidates

From your first application to your first day as an apprentice.

  1. Already done

    You apply to a participating employer

    You applied for an apprenticeship with BAE Systems or Babcock and reached the agreed stage of their recruitment process.

  2. You’re invited to opt in

    If you weren’t offered a role, you can choose to join the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge. It’s your decision — there’s no obligation.

  3. Create your profile on Workplus

    Complete a profile on the Workplus platform telling employers about your interests, location and travel preferences.

  4. May 2027

    Explore opportunities and apply

    Browse the approved apprenticeship opportunities and apply to the ones that suit you.

  5. May – June 2027

    Shortlisting and interviews

    Employers review applications and interview candidates within the coordinated campaign timetable.

  6. Get your offer and start your apprenticeship

    If you’re successful, your new employer and their learning provider complete the normal employment and apprenticeship enrolment arrangements with you.

Make the most of it

Top tips for candidates

A few simple things can make a big difference when applications open.

  • Keep your Workplus profile complete and up to date.
  • Be realistic about how far you can travel each day — it helps you find the right match.
  • Keep an open mind: apprenticeships exist in many sectors, from engineering and manufacturing to business and digital.
  • Check your emails (and junk folder) so you don’t miss invitations or interview dates.
  • Research each employer before your interview and think about why the role interests you.
Questions

Candidate FAQs

Do I have to join the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge?
No. Eligible candidates are invited to opt in, and it’s completely your choice.
Am I guaranteed an apprenticeship?
No. You choose which opportunities to apply for, and taking part doesn’t guarantee an interview, offer or appointment. Each employer assesses every applicant fairly against the requirements published for its role and makes its own decisions.
Where are the opportunities?
The pilot focuses on the areas around Barrow-in-Furness and Preston (BAE Systems) and Devonport (Babcock). You choose opportunities based on your location and travel preferences.
When can I apply?
Applications open in May 2027. Employers then shortlist and interview during May and June 2027.
I didn’t apply to BAE Systems or Babcock. Can I still take part?
This pilot is designed for eligible candidates from the participating employers’ recruitment processes. Keep an eye on the Workplus campaign page for any updates.
What is an apprenticeship?
An apprenticeship is a paid job with a structured programme of training. As an apprentice you are employed, work towards an approved apprenticeship standard and receive your training during paid time, delivered with a learning provider.
Will I be paid?
Yes. Apprentices must be paid at least the statutory minimum that applies to their age and apprenticeship status, including time spent training. Each employer sets out its salary and terms in its vacancy.
Will I need to complete a separate application with a training provider?
Sometimes, where a learning provider genuinely needs one for course assessment, eligibility or enrolment. If so, its purpose and timing should be explained clearly and coordinated with the employer’s application.
Who checks whether I’m eligible for an apprenticeship?
The learning provider completes the formal eligibility, prior-learning and funding checks with you and the employer. Taking part in the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge doesn’t by itself confirm funding eligibility.
