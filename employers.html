Why take part

What the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge offers your organisation

Whether you already employ apprentices or are exploring apprenticeships for the first time, the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge can help you connect a genuine opportunity with the right people.

Pre-engaged applicants

Candidates have already reached an agreed stage in a competitive major-employer apprenticeship process.

No fee to take part

There is no participation fee. Normal employment and apprenticeship costs, such as pay and any training contribution, remain the employer’s responsibility.

You stay in control

It complements your existing recruitment. You own your vacancy, shortlisting, interviews and appointment decisions.

Local talent

Candidates choose opportunities based on their location and travel preferences — people who want to work in your area.

Support to shape your role

Work with your learning provider and the pilot team to prepare a viable, high-quality apprenticeship opportunity.

A coordinated campaign

A clear, time-bound recruitment window — not a rolling jobs board — so effort is focused when it matters.

Your commitment

What participation involves

Your commitment is the time and practical support needed to prepare an opportunity, support candidates and work within the campaign timetable.

Please note: participation does not guarantee applications or an appointment.

  • Have a genuine role and the capacity to employ, supervise and support an apprentice.
  • Work with your learning provider and the pilot team to prepare a viable opportunity.
  • Confirm the role, location, relevant apprenticeship standard, entry requirements and proposed start arrangements.
  • Agree suitable training arrangements and the required eligibility and funding checks with your provider.
  • Prepare your opportunity to the agreed local timetable.
  • Run a fair, inclusive and timely recruitment process and keep applicants informed.
  • Complete normal employment arrangements and share agreed outcomes to support the pilot’s evaluation.

Learning providers

Extend the reach of your provision

The Apprenticeship Talent Bridge lets learning providers promote relevant apprenticeship provision to new employers, and offer employers they already support an additional, coordinated recruitment stream.

Existing employer–provider relationships remain in place. The campaign doesn’t replace the provider’s role in training delivery, funding guidance, employer support or enrolment. There is no fee for learning providers to take part.

Questions

Employer FAQs

Is there a cost to take part?
There is no fee for employers to participate. Your commitment is the time and practical support needed to develop a genuine opportunity, work with the relevant learning provider, recruit within the campaign timetable and support the successful apprentice. Normal employment and apprenticeship costs, including pay and any applicable training contribution, remain the employer’s responsibility.
What makes a role an apprenticeship?
An apprenticeship is a paid job with a structured programme of training. The apprentice must be employed, work towards an approved English apprenticeship standard and receive the required training during paid time. The employer and learning provider must put the apprenticeship agreement and training plan in place before or at the start of the apprenticeship.
Do we need to have recruited apprentices before?
No. Employers new to apprenticeships can register. You will need a genuine role, the capacity to employ and support an apprentice, and a suitable training route. The campaign team and relevant learning provider can help you understand role design, funding, supervision and the steps required before recruitment.
Do we need an existing learning provider?
No. If you already work with a provider, that relationship will be recorded and respected. If you do not, you can be supported to explore suitable provision. The employer remains responsible for selecting a provider and agreeing the training arrangements.
What must be agreed before our opportunity is published?
You will need to confirm the job and duties, apprenticeship standard, provider, location and working arrangements, salary, hours, entry requirements, funding route and anticipated start. The role must provide enough relevant work and support for the apprentice to develop the knowledge, skills and behaviours required by the standard.
What are our responsibilities as the apprentice’s employer?
You employ the apprentice and are responsible for the employment contract, pay, a safe working environment, supervision and fair treatment. You must allow the apprentice to complete the required training during paid working time, take part in progress reviews and work with the provider to support successful completion.
How should salary and employment terms be set?
The apprentice must be paid at least the statutory minimum that applies to their age and apprenticeship status, including paid time spent training. Offer fair, clearly stated terms, check the rates in force at the intended start date, and explain any salary progression, working pattern, probation and benefits in the vacancy.
Who decides whether a candidate is eligible for apprenticeship funding?
The learning provider completes the formal eligibility, prior-learning and funding checks with the employer and candidate. Employers should not promise funded training until those checks are complete. A candidate may still be suitable for the job but need a different training or funding arrangement.
Can we use our normal recruitment process?
Yes. The Apprenticeship Talent Bridge complements your recruitment process. You remain responsible for fair shortlisting, interviews, reasonable adjustments, right-to-work checks and the final appointment decision, while working within the shared campaign timetable and keeping candidates informed.
Are applications, interviews or appointments guaranteed?
No. Candidates choose which opportunities to apply for, and registration does not guarantee applications, interviews, offers or an appointment. Employers must assess each applicant fairly against the published requirements.
What happens after we make an offer?
The employer, successful candidate and provider confirm employment, eligibility, funding and training arrangements. The required apprenticeship documents are completed, the training and assessment approach is agreed, and the provider completes enrolment before the apprenticeship begins.

Learning providers can find their own FAQs on the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge campaign page on Workplus.
