The opportunity

A positive onward route

Major defence employers attract more strong apprenticeship applicants than they have places available. At the same time, other employers in their local communities can struggle to attract the people and skills they need.

The Apprenticeship Talent Bridge helps suitable applicants who reach an agreed point in a participating employer’s recruitment process — but are not offered a role — discover other relevant opportunities close to home.

Campaign timeline

More than a vacancy listing

The Apprenticeship Talent Bridge is a time-bound, coordinated recruitment campaign — not a rolling jobs board. Opportunities are prepared first, so every candidate gets a clearer, more consistent journey.

1
Until March 2027

Employers sign up and create apprenticeship opportunities, with support and training arrangements in place.

2
May 2027

Eligible candidates explore the approved opportunities and submit their applications.

3
May – June 2027

Employers shortlist and interview within the coordinated campaign timetable.

How it works

Crossing the bridge, step by step

  1. Employers & learning providers register

    A short expression-of-interest form captures contact details, followed by a more detailed form tailored to their role in the campaign.

  2. Opportunities are developed

    By March 2027, employers are supported to shape genuine apprenticeship roles and confirm the right training route.

  3. Eligible candidates opt in

    Suitable candidates who reach the agreed stage of the participating employer’s recruitment process can choose to join the Apprenticeship Talent Bridge.

  4. Applications open in May 2027

    Candidates complete a profile and choose the approved opportunities that suit their interests, location and travel preferences.

  5. Employers recruit in May & June

    Shortlisting and interviews run through a fair process within the coordinated timetable.

  6. Training & outcomes confirmed

    Successful candidates, employers and learning providers complete the normal apprenticeship and enrolment arrangements.

Free to take part. There is no participation fee for employers or learning providers. Your commitment is the time and practical support needed to prepare opportunities, support candidates and work within the coordinated campaign timetable.
